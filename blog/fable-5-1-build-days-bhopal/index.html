The night Bhopal trended on X

Claude announced Fable 5.1 Build Days in cities around the world. One Indian city made the list. The timeline had opinions, and we saved all of them.

  • 259 posts in the trend
  • 129 name Bhopal
  • 598.3K views on the announcement

What happened

At 1:27 am on Thursday, @claudeai posted a 30-second video. Fable 5.1 Build Days, a worldwide buildathon from September 11 to 25, with the Claude community hosting in cities across every continent. The video scrolls through the host cities. Nairobi. Stockholm. Cape Town. Oslo. Mexico City. Osaka. Miami. Then, in a clay-coloured box: Bhopal.

Bhopal is the only Indian city on the list. No Bengaluru, no Delhi, no Mumbai, no Hyderabad. Within half an hour the quote-posts started, and they did not stop. By 5:37 am X's trending system had bundled the conversation into its own story page, first titled "Claude Fable 5.1 Build Days Launch Worldwide with Bhopal Spotlight" and later renamed "Claude's Fable 5.1 Buildathons Pick Bhopal as India's Only Host City". That page is where this post comes from.

Anthropic launched worldwide buildathons from September 11 to 25 to experiment with Fable 5.1, its strongest model yet for long-running coding and knowledge tasks. In India, organizer Aniket Sahu hosts a full-day session in Bhopal on September 20, surprising the tech scene and drawing mixed reactions of humor, appreciation for smaller cities, and light-hearted jabs at skipping bigger hubs. Community buzz highlights the event as a chance to tackle local problems with AI, amid a global rollout across Asia-Pacific, Africa, Europe, and the Americas.

X's summary of the trend, written by Grok. X adds: "This story is a summary of posts on X and may evolve over time. Grok can make mistakes, verify its outputs."

Why Bhopal?

Because someone here put an event on the calendar. Build Days are hosted by local Claude community chapters, and the Bhopal chapter has been meeting since July. Eight meetups so far, all free, all run by volunteers. When the Fable 5.1 Build Days went up on the global calendar, Bhopal was on it.

Three events are now on the way, and the Build Day is the one the whole timeline was arguing about.
