How could AI make Bhopal better?

You form a team on the spot, prototype a solution with Claude, and demo it to a judging panel by evening. Possible challenge areas:

Citizen services Public information Mobility & transport Healthcare access Education Waste & cleanliness Water & environment Tourism

✻ good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. The Impact Lab on Sunday, August 23 is free to attend. Entry is by approval, so register early on Luma and we'll confirm your spot.

Do I need to know how to code?

No — Impact Lab teams are multidisciplinary on purpose. Developers prototype with Claude, while designers, students, researchers and people who simply know the city shape the problem, the user experience and the pitch. Every team needs both kinds of people.

Do I need a team before I register?

No. Team formation is part of the morning — come solo and we'll match you into a multidisciplinary team. Coming with friends works too; just have everyone register individually.

What should I bring?

A charged laptop and your charger — it's a full day. Teams work with public, non-confidential data only, so no special access is needed. Ideas and opinions about how Bhopal actually works are as valuable as code.

I missed the earlier meetups — can I still come?

Of course. Every event stands on its own, and teams form on the day, so new faces slot right in. Register, show up, and you'll have a team by noon.

Where in Bhopal is it?

We share the exact venue with confirmed registrants a day or two before, once your Luma registration is approved. Keep an eye on your email.

Can I bring a friend?

Please do. Just have them register on Luma too, so we can plan seating for the right number of people.
