Yes. All three September events are free; entry is by approval on Luma, so register early.

No. Impact Lab teams mix developers with designers, students and people who know the city.

What should I bring?

A charged laptop and charger. Public data only — no special access needed.

Sep 2: AIC-RNTU Foundation, Chiklod Road. Sep 12–13: shared with approved registrants by email.
